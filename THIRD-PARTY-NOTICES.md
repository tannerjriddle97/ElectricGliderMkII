# Dependencies and asset references

Vintage Story is developed by Anego Studios. Electrical Progressive is developed by tehtelev, Kotl, Auto_Yoba, and its contributors. This is an unofficial, separate add-on.

The mod references Electrical Progressive's installed armor texture and glider sound, and Vintage Story's metal/coal textures, by asset location. Those media assets and original mod DLLs are not bundled. The four Mk II models are original cuboid geometry generated by this add-on's scripts; they use the game's UpperTorso attachment convention and do not copy EP model geometry. Vintage Story API/game DLLs, Harmony, Newtonsoft.Json, and protobuf-net are supplied by the installed game and are not redistributed in this package. The source package contains this add-on's source, geometry, configuration, documentation, tests and authoring scripts.

Locally generated model-preview artifacts embed installed dependency textures for private review. They remain under ignored artifacts/ and are not included in either distributable archive.
